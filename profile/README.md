**One control room for the hall, the meeting and the mics.** Spectacast runs live hybrid events:
every hall's OBS, the online meeting with its remote speakers, the NDI feeds and the microphones,
from one dashboard.

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Spectacast Director** | The engine and dashboard: OBS, NDI, the meeting and the mics in one place, with rules for the routine. |
| **Spectacast Meet** | Joins the meeting and gives every participant and share its own NDI feed. |
| **Spectacast Mic** | Phones as wireless mics and talkback, received on the PC as audio devices. |
